# CapRocket 0.1.0: first public release 🚀

A free, open-source desktop video editor in the style of CapCut, for Windows.
No account, no subscription, no watermark: every feature is free.

## Install

1. Download **`CapRocket-Setup-0.1.0.exe`** below (Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit, about 120 MB).
2. Run it, choose the install folder, finish. CapRocket is added to the Desktop and the Start menu.
3. Windows SmartScreen may show *"Windows protected your PC"* because the installer is not code-signed yet:
   click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Auto captions and text-to-speech voices download their models the first time you use them.

## What's inside

**Editing**
- CapCut-style timeline: magnetic main track, overlay (picture-in-picture), text, stickers, effects and audio tracks
- Razor tool, split, delete left / right (Q / W), markers, snapping, ripple magnet per track, undo / redo
- Move, resize and rotate clips right in the player; right-click menu with copy / paste attributes, extract audio and more

**Picture**
- Transform, opacity, 13 blend modes, chroma key (green screen), masks, filters and adjustments, speed 0.25× to 4×

**Effects and transitions**
- Animated effects: VHS Noise, Striped Erosion, BW Grit, Zoom Blast, Snow Storm, Limelight, Fire Burn, Burning Glow
- Transitions: Mosaic Wipe, Snap Back, Flare Wipe, Comic Cut, Brush Cuts, Phone Reveal, Finger Swipe, and the classics
- Put an effect on a clip, or free on the Effects track and stretch it over several clips
- GPU rendering: what you see in the player is what you export

**Text, captions and voice**
- Auto captions with Whisper, running offline (Arabic including Darija, French, English, Spanish…)
- Caption templates (Hormozi, Karaoke, Word Box, Neon, Arabic Bold…) with word-by-word highlights
- Text to speech: free voices on your PC (Piper) or ElevenLabs with your own key

**Libraries**
- Music (Jamendo) and sound effects (SFXMint, Freesound), only sounds free to use in videos, plus 20 offline effects
- Pixabay stock videos and photos (free API key)
- Favorites kept across projects

**AI assistant**
- RocketPilot: ask *"remove the silences"*, *"add captions in Arabic"*, *"make it 9:16"*… (Google Gemini key)

**Export**
- MP4 from 480p to 4K, any aspect ratio, 24 to 60 fps; audio only as MP3, WAV or M4A

## Good to know
- Keys (Gemini, Pixabay, ElevenLabs) are optional and stored encrypted on your PC.
- Videos the player cannot decode smoothly (10-bit, HEVC, above 1080p) get a light preview copy; the export uses the original.
- CapRocket is an independent project, not affiliated with CapCut or ByteDance.
